What cell phone problems can be repaired?

Common repair issues include cracked screens, battery problems, charging ports, camera faults, speaker problems, microphone issues, and certain water-related damage.

Is it better to repair or replace a damaged phone?

It depends on the phone's age, condition, value, and type of damage. A professional diagnosis can help you understand whether repair is a practical option.

Can a cracked phone screen be replaced?

Many cracked screens can be replaced. The appropriate repair depends on the phone model and the condition of the display and other components.
